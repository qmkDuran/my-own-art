# Night Town: "Zelda meets Destiny" in a cozy-spooky world

## One line
A cozy-spooky action adventure: your always-night town is home; you head out on missions into haunted places, fight with flashy skills, solve Zelda-style puzzles, find secrets, craft gear from regional materials, and grow your class through skill trees and sets.

## Pillars
1. **Combat that looks and feels amazing.** You control and aim everything (no auto-fire). Flashy, colourful skills in the style of Final Fantasy XIV, Genshin Impact and Kingdom Hearts: bold shapes, motion and colour, glowing but never harsh.
2. **Missions and places, Destiny-style.** Head out to distinct areas (woods, shore, crypts, marsh) for missions: fight packs of minions and elites, reach a boss, get rewarded.
3. **Zelda puzzles and secrets.** Dungeons with puzzles, hidden rooms, shortcuts, and rewards for curiosity.
4. **Deep progression, Last Epoch/Grim Dawn-style.** A class, skill trees, levels, and builds that change how you play.
5. **Meaningful loot, not loot spam.** Drops are rare and exciting. Gear sets (like The Division and Destiny) boost a specific build or skill tree.
6. **Crafting from places.** Each region has its own materials; you craft and upgrade gear in town.
7. **A lively home town.** Shops that matter, townsfolk with routines, music, small life everywhere.
8. **The vibe.** Always night, moody lighting, cozy-spooky (never horror). Spooky-cute enemies.

## Class (start with one)
- **The Lamplighter (caster):** light and fire magic from a lantern.
- Later, possible classes:
  - a melee **Bellwarden** (a heavy bell-hammer, shockwaves)
  - a **Moth Dancer** (agile daggers, summons)
  - a **Hexsmith** (traps and constructs)

## Combat model (inspired by Destiny's ability structure)
- **Primary:** an aimed, rapid lantern bolt (always available; the bread and butter).
- **Heavy:** a charged attack (hold to charge, release a big beam or burst).
- **Grenade-like ability:** a thrown lantern bomb (an area burst) on a cooldown.
- **Class ability:** a blink/dash and a defensive ward.
- **Super:** charges up from fighting; a huge flashy ultimate (e.g. "Sunrise": a giant blazing lantern sun that rains fire).
- **Movement:** jump, dodge, and good camera; lock-on optional.
- **Enemies:** readable telegraphs, roles (swarmers, shooters, tanks, elites), and bosses with phases.

## Progression
- Levels, then skill points, then a skill tree per class (branches that change how abilities work).
- Gear slots (weapon/lantern, coat, hat, charm, boots, ring).
- Rarity: common, rare, legendary, set.
- Sets give a big bonus to one build.
- Drops are uncommon; bosses and chests give the best rewards; crafting fills the gaps.

## Build order (one thing at a time, playtest each)
1. **Combat core:** one mission area just outside town; the Lamplighter's full kit (primary, heavy, bomb, dash, super) with Final Fantasy XIV/Genshin-level effects; 3 enemy types plus an elite plus a mini boss; music. Playtest: is the combat fun and flashy?
2. **Skill tree and levelling** for the Lamplighter.
3. **Loot, gear and one set.**
4. **Regional materials and crafting** in town.
5. **First Zelda-style dungeon** with puzzles and secrets.
6. **Town life:** shops, more townsfolk, routines, requests.
7. **More regions and missions**, then more classes.
