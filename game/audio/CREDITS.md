# Audio credits

Most sounds and both music loops are synthesised from code (`art/audio/synth.py`, made for this game, CC0).
Combat, creature and UI sounds are layered (`art/audio/layer.py`) from the code-made layers plus these CC0
(public-domain dedication) recordings. The originals are kept unchanged in `art/audio/cc0/`. CC0 needs no
attribution; it is given here anyway. Each licence was checked on its OpenGameArt page on 2026-11-11 (the page's
licence field reads "CC0", linking to creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
